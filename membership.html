A few things worth knowing.

Can I keep watching for free?

Yes. Newbie includes regular Objectflix content, a personal watchlist, local viewing history, basic profiles, and public Movie Nights. A paid plan is optional.

How do I change or cancel a membership?

Use Manage membership to open Stripe's Customer Portal. Stripe shows the price and billing effect before you confirm a change. Cancellation timing follows the policy displayed in the portal. Your access updates after Stripe verifies the change.

Why do I need Google or Discord for membership?

The original email accounts in this copy are saved only in your browser. Membership and public community actions need a server-verified identity. Google and Discord provide that verification without treating a browser profile as proof of payment.

Is a donation the same as a membership?

No. Any one-time donation or contribution is separate from a recurring membership and does not grant paid perks. Contributions are not described as charitable donations.

Are all proposed perks available?

No. The comparison lists features that are implemented and currently enabled. Possible future perks are listed separately and are not included in your purchase.
